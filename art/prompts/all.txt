Red / Fire — #D3082F

A raw uncut gemstone in deep crimson, unfaceted and rough, resting in a bed of
live embers on cracked black basalt. Palette: #D3082F the stone, #F26E31 ember
accent, #2B050C the dark ground.

Style reference — fetch and match these two images:
https://ashtonkellis.github.io/might-and-magic/art/seeds/emerald-gemstone.jpg
https://ashtonkellis.github.io/might-and-magic/art/seeds/amethyst-gemstone.jpg

Match their rendering exactly: polished photoreal digital fantasy art, not
painting, with a shallow depth of field that throws the background into soft
blur and bokeh. The gemstone is the only light source — it glows from within
and casts its colour onto everything near it. The environment behind is that
element's own habitat, heavily out of focus and tinted the same colour, dark
enough that the stone is the brightest thing in frame. Change only the object
and the colour; keep the finish, the lighting logic and the depth of field.

Landscape 3:2. The object is centred and fills about 40% of the frame. Full
bleed to all four edges. No text, lettering, numerals, signatures, watermarks,
borders, frames or card templates. No people, hands, faces or creatures. Keep
nothing essential within the outer 4% of the left and right edges.
