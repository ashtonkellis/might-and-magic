The Long Rehearsal

SUBJECT: The same scene staged over and over, receding into the depth of the plate -- rank after rank of identical figures in identical attitudes, each one a fraction further through the same movement, running back until the hatching can no longer resolve them. The largest and most repetitive image in the class. Somewhere in the middle ranks one figure is out of step, and nobody has noticed.

CHRONOMANCER HOUSE STYLE -- identical on every card in this class, do not vary it.

Medium: fine engraved line and crosshatch modelling, in the manner of an
18th-century scientific plate. No painterly texture anywhere, no digital
airbrush, no soft gradients -- every value is built from the density of ruled
and cross-ruled line.

Light: flat, even and sourceless. There is no lamp, no sun and no cast shadow.
Form comes only from how tightly the hatching sits.

Composition, and this is the class: every figure is printed three times in
slight registration offset. One cyan-shifted copy sits a few millimetres behind,
one amber-shifted copy a few millimetres ahead, and the true sepia figure is
sharp between them. The ghosts are misregistration, not motion blur -- crisp
line work, just off-plate. The subject is standing in three moments at once.

Ground: pale. This is the only class in the game printed on a light field, and
against the rest of the set it should read as a page torn from a different,
older book. Plate white #F0EBE0 throughout, sepia ink #4A3A28 for the line,
registration cyan #7FB2C4 behind and registration amber #F5B202 ahead. Nothing
saturated, nothing dark -- no black, no filled shadow masses.

Plate furniture: faint ruled margins, engraved tick marks and the ghost of an
annotation in the corners, as a scientific plate carries. Never legible as
words -- suggestion of notation only, never actual letters or numbers.

Register: boredom. Nobody in this class is surprised by anything. Where other
classes react, these faces have already sat through it. Too smooth, too still,
expressions arriving a beat late or not at all.

FORMAT: landscape 3:2. Full bleed to all four edges. No text, lettering,
numerals, signatures, watermarks, borders, frames or card templates -- the card
frame is drawn separately and this image sits inside it. Keep nothing essential
within the outer 4% of the left and right edges.
