SUBJECT: An action shot -- Caine mid-attack, the moment the fire leaves him.
He is lunging forward and low, one arm whipped across his body and the other
thrown out ahead, hurling a fistful of torn burning pages that are erupting into
a firestorm as they go. Coat and hair dragged backward by the motion. The blast
goes up and out of the top of the frame, so the fire owns the upper two thirds
and he is driving into the lower third from the corner. Caught at the fastest
point of the movement, not before or after it. He is laughing into it.

This is the most spectacular thing he does and the portrait format exists to
carry the eruption, so use the height.

FORMAT: full art, portrait 2:3. The image is the entire card face -- nothing
will be printed over it and no frame will crop it, so it must carry the card by
itself. Bleed to all four edges. No text, lettering, numerals, signatures,
watermarks, borders or frames. Keep nothing essential within the outer 4% of any
edge.

Two deliberate departures from the class style, and only these two: the fire is
ahead of and above him rather than behind, because he is throwing it; and the
composition is a hard diagonal driving out of one corner rather than a figure
with air around it. Everything else -- the mid-key value, the dark shape in the
hot field, no environment, lost-and-found edges, the palette, the phoenix
resolving out of the flame -- holds exactly.

PYROMANCER HOUSE STYLE -- identical on every card in this class, do not vary it.

Medium: smooth, blended digital painting, rendered and illustrative, with no
visible paint texture. Detail concentrates in the face and falls off outward --
the flame stays loose and gestural while the face is the only fully resolved
thing in frame.

Light: fire is the only light source and it sits inside the frame, behind and
beside the subject. Rim-light along hair, shoulder and forearm; the subject's
front stays in warm shadow.

Value: mid-key, not low-key. Near-black is all but absent and the bulk of the
image holds the midrange. The figure is a dark shape in a hot bright field,
never a lit shape in darkness. At thumbnail size it must read as a dark
silhouette on a glowing ground.

Composition: no environment whatsoever. Flame and smoke are the entire
background and all depth comes from value. Dynamic diagonal, weight off-centre,
caught mid-gesture.

Edges: lost-and-found. Hair and shoulders dissolve into the flame; the
silhouette is never fully closed.

Palette: no pure black and no saturated cadmium red. The dominant notes are
warm-neutral browns and oxide reds -- #4B1E17, #903827, #A2503C, #CA6938,
#D38C4A, #936151, #EDD19B -- and that restraint is what lets the few bright
spots read as heat.

SIGNATURE, required on every card: a phoenix head resolves out of the fire
behind the subject's shoulder, at low contrast, visible only on a second look.
Never obvious, never absent.

Register: joy. This class laughs. Not smirking, not grim -- openly,
inappropriately delighted, even mid-catastrophe.
